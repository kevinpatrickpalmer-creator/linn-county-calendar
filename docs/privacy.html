Privacy Policy

Plain language, not legalese — this describes exactly what this site does with information, nothing more and nothing less.

What we collect, and why

  • If you submit an event: the event details, plus your contact email and/or phone number. That contact info is shown publicly on the calendar listing, on purpose, so people with questions about the event can reach you directly. We don't call you or use it for anything else.
  • If you sign up for reminder emails or the newsletter: your email address, which opt-ins you checked, and any towns you chose to filter by. Used only to send the emails you actually opted into.
  • If you just subscribe to the calendar on your phone or computer's calendar app: nothing personal is collected at all. That's a direct connection between your device and the calendar file — we don't know who's subscribed.
  • Basic site usage: anonymous pageview counts via Cloudflare Web Analytics — no cookies, no personal identifiers, just aggregate visit numbers.

Where this goes

We use a few outside services to run this site, and each only sees the specific piece of information it needs to do its job:

  • Event submissions are emailed to the admin for review via Web3Forms.
  • Reminder/newsletter sign-ups are stored in a Google Form and Sheet.
  • Reminder emails are sent through Brevo.
  • The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which — like any web host — keeps standard technical access logs.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or trade your information to anyone.
  • We don't call or text you using contact info submitted for an event listing.
  • We don't use your email for anything beyond what you specifically opted into.

Public calendar content

Some of what appears on the calendar isn't submitted by anyone directly — it's aggregated from other public sources (city/county government calendars, school athletic schedules, local funeral homes' published obituaries, etc.) to build a single, complete community calendar. That content reflects what those sources already publish publicly; we don't collect it from the individuals it's about.

Your choices

  • To stop reminder/newsletter emails, use the unsubscribe link included in every email, or go directly to unsubscribe.html.
  • To stop seeing events on your phone or computer, remove the subscribed calendar in your device's own calendar app settings.
  • Questions about a specific listing, or anything else here — contact us using the address below.
